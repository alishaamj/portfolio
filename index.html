📖 About Me

Welcome to my Minecraft World! My name is Alisha Amjad, and I'm a senior studying Cybersecurity at Purdue University and a Tier 1 SOC Analyst at Purdue's Security Operations Center.

I like to create case studies based on different websites and games, finding security gaps or identifying security features I'd want to implement. I also enjoy experimenting with and researching various LLMs to test their functionality.

The part of security I enjoy most is the investigative side: triaging alerts, finding the weak spots in a system or network, and tracing them back to see how they could be exploited. I'm especially interested in security consulting and GRC, and I have experience working with frameworks like NIST CSF and ISO 27001/27002 to turn technical risk into actionable recommendations.

I am passionate about cybersecurity, and eager to learn!
